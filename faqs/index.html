Getting started

Start with what matters to you.

What is Habitual Growth?

Habitual Growth is a life management system that moves you toward what matters. It brings your priorities, Goals, Essential Actions, and progress into one system, so your effort has a clear direction. Do More. Be More. Live More.

Who is Habitual Growth for?

Habitual Growth is for people ready to turn intention into action: parents, professionals, students, entrepreneurs, and anyone working toward meaningful personal growth. Start with the part of your life that matters most to you.

How do I get started with Habitual Growth?

Download Habitual Growth for iPhone or Android and create your account. Choose something that matters to you and give it a place in your day with an Essential Action. You can begin with Starter, the free plan.

Do I need a clear goal before I start?

No. You can begin with an action you want to follow through on today. As your direction becomes clearer, define a Goal and connect the actions that help you move toward it.

Is Habitual Growth available for iPhone and Android?

Yes. Download Habitual Growth from the Apple App Store for iPhone or Google Play for Android. The website helps you explore the product; use the mobile app to work with your Goals and Essential Actions.

Daily actions & progress

Give your priorities a place in your day.

What are Essential Actions?

Essential Actions are the concrete steps you choose to move what matters forward. They can support a Goal, a habit, or an important priority. Schedule them for the days you intend to act and mark them complete as you follow through.

How do Goals and Essential Actions work together?

A Goal names the outcome you want. Essential Actions are what you do to move toward it. For example, preparing for a race is a Goal; completing today’s training session is an Essential Action.

What are Pending Actions and Upcoming Actions?

Pending Actions give you a place to keep actions that need attention beyond your current daily focus. In the app, you create these as Upcoming Actions with a scheduled date and a high, medium, or low priority. Review and filter them so important commitments stay in view.

What are Proven Behaviors?

Proven Behaviors are the actions you recognize as worth continuing because they support your progress. Habitual Growth gives these behaviors a place alongside your habits and Essential Actions. Use your own experience and progress history to decide what to build on.

How can I see my progress over time?

Habitual Growth brings your completed Essential Actions, habits, and progress views together so you can review your consistency. Use that history to reflect on what is working and what deserves your attention next. Available views depend on your plan.

What if I miss a day or my priorities change?

Return to what matters now. Review your actions and adjust your schedule or commitments to fit your current priorities. Growth is built through repeated effort over time; one missed day does not have to determine your next step.

Challenges & Plans

Structure for the direction you choose.

What is the difference between a Challenge and a Plan?

A Challenge is a focused commitment over a defined period. A Plan is a structured path toward a Goal, with milestones and activities that break the journey into manageable steps. Choose a Challenge to focus your effort or a Plan to work toward a longer-term outcome.

How do I find Challenges and Plans that fit my goals?

Explore Challenges and Plans in the app and review their descriptions, activities, and duration. Choose the structure that fits what you want to work toward and the time you can commit. Access depends on your subscription plan.

How do Challenges and Plans connect to daily action?

Challenges and Plans organize a commitment into activities you can follow through on. Rather than leaving your goal as an idea, they give you specific steps to work on and a structure for reviewing progress.

Communities & households

Move forward with people who matter.

Can I grow with other people in Habitual Growth?

Yes. Communities bring people together around shared interests and commitments. Groups and shared Challenges give you ways to participate with others while continuing to work toward your own Goals.

Can I create a Community or join a Group?

Starter members can join Groups when invited. Growth includes creating or joining up to 10 Groups, and Professional includes the Growth benefits. In the app, Groups are how you take part in these Communities.

What are household subaccounts?

Subaccounts let additional people in your household participate through your account structure. Growth includes one free subaccount and access to two additional paid subaccounts. Professional includes four free subaccounts and access to one additional paid subaccount.

Do I need to join a Community to use Habitual Growth?

No. You can work independently with your own Goals and Essential Actions. Communities and shared Challenges are options you choose when growing alongside others fits your life.

Sequoia AI

There when you want it.

What is Sequoia AI?

Sequoia AI is the personalized, supportive engine inside Habitual Growth. It helps you think through your Goals, shape a Plan, and work on the actions that move you forward. You stay in charge of the direction.

Do I have to use Sequoia AI?

No. You can use Habitual Growth’s Goals, Essential Actions, and other available plan features without engaging with Sequoia. Technology when you need it. Gone when you don’t.

Can Sequoia AI help me build a Plan?

Yes. On an eligible plan, you can share what you want to work toward, answer questions about your Goal, and use Sequoia to generate a Plan with milestones and activities. Review the result and decide how it fits your life.

What are Sequoia AI credits?

Credits are used for Sequoia AI features. Growth includes 100 free credits and Professional includes 500 free credits. Usage can vary by the action and request; check your balance and the information shown in the app before using a feature.
